
# How to test Creator Launchpad

If you are here from Stardance to rate my project, hello first of all!

The app is still in closed testing, so in order to access my demo:

1. Join the Google Group with the link below, it will first say you don't have permission, but then you should click to join the group. You don't have to link to your Google profile or put any subscriptions since this group is just so you can access the closed testing. To double check you have actually joined the group, you should see *My Membership Settings* pop up below *About* on the left.

- [Google Group Link](https://groups.google.com/g/creator-launchpad-testers)
   
2. Then opt in to the closed test to download the demo using the link below.

- [Closed Test Link](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.NexBlade_Studios.Creator_Launchpad)

**Once my project has been rated you WILL be removed from the Google Group and the group will be deleted.** You can always just leave the group whenever you have finished testing the app.
